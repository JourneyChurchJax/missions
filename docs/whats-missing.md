# What's missing (as of October 1, 2026)

The preview has every page, but it only shows sample data. Below is everything still needed to fully replace ManagedMissions, plus what would make travelers feel at ease.

## 1. ManagedMissions features we still need

### Trips
- Create a trip (step-by-step), copy last year's trip, edit every trip setting
- Cancel or postpone a trip, auto-archive 30 days after return
- Groups of trips (by region or ministry)
- Max team size, application deadline, partner organization

### Team
- Add people to a trip, set role (admin, leader, traveler), "not traveling" flag
- Invite emails, password reset, two-step sign-in
- Full person profile: passport, emergency contacts, medical, diet, T-shirt
- Merge duplicate people, tags

### Tasks and goals
- Six task types: traveler, leader, admin, data check, upload a document, staff-wide
- Minors-only tasks, tasks by tag, attachments on tasks
- Fundraising goals by date (% or $), scheduled reminder emails
- "Email everyone who hasn't done this"

### Meetings
- Create meetings for one or many trips, location, notes
- Take attendance, calendar view, calendar subscription

### Budget and money
- Budget lines with "quantity = team size", per-person goal math, fundraising exceptions
- Expenses with currency and exchange rate, budget vs spent
- Enter checks and cash, deposits and deposit reports, refunds
- Import gifts and people from spreadsheets, CSV exports
- Donor records, merge donors, monthly givers, donor login, year-end statements

### Applications
- Form builder with custom questions
- References (send, collect, read)
- Application deposits, early-bird and discount codes
- Review queue: approve to a trip, decline, download answers

### Public pages
- Each traveler's fundraising page (approve before public, QR code, share)
- Team giving page, trip story or updates page
- Public list of open trips (apply or give)

### Communication
- Email the team or a filtered group, saved templates with fill-in fields
- In-app messages with history

### Connections
- Stripe (cards, Apple Pay, bank, monthly, donor covers fees)
- Planning Center (sign-in, people, giving)
- Background checks (Checkr), e-signatures, AFC Travel flight sync

## 2. What would make travelers feel at ease (beyond ManagedMissions)

- **Trip guide:** where we sleep (address), what we eat, in-country contacts, what to wear, packing list, money and exchange, weather, power plugs, phone and Wi-Fi, safety plan
- **Flights per person:** confirmation number, flight times, bag rules, airport meeting spot and time
- **Health and entry:** vaccines, travel insurance card, visa or entry rules, passport checklist
- **Hour-by-hour itinerary**, and a "Today" view during the trip
- **Printable trip packet** (PDF) that works offline
- **Parents and guardians:** their own view for minors, consent forms, travel-day updates
- **Family updates:** "we landed" texts and emails to families during the trip
- **Training:** short videos, devotionals, prayer guide, FAQ
- **Team roster with photos** so people know each other before they go
- **After the trip:** debrief, survey, shared photos

## 3. What admins need to post all of that

- One place to upload flights per person (or for the group) and travel documents
- Mark a document "everyone must read" and see who has opened or signed it
- Announcements by text, email and in-app
- Rooming list and van or seat assignments
- Emergency sheet: every traveler's contacts and medical info, printable for leaders
- Travel-day check-in ("I'm at the airport")
- Trip payments from travelers: deposits, payment plans, "pay for my own trip"
- Incident log during the trip, and an activity history of who changed what

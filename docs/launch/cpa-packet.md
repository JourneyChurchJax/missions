# Journey Missions: gift setup for CPA review

Draft prepared October 2, 2026 for Journey Church's CPA. Please mark up anything that should change. Nothing goes live for online giving until you sign off.

## Who we are

- **Organization:** Journey Church (legal name and EIN to confirm), 6225-2 Lake Gray Blvd, Jacksonville, FL 32244
- **What this is:** our own website for mission trips (missions.journeychurch.org). It replaces ManagedMissions, which we used before.
- **Trips this season:** Belize (June 19–25, 2027, $1,900 per traveler) and Israel (May 19–28, 2027).

## How money comes in

| Type | Who pays | How it's recorded | Receipt? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gift toward a traveler** | Friends, family, church members | Gift to Journey Church, with the traveler's name noted as a *preference* | Yes, year-end statement |
| **Gift toward a whole trip team** | Anyone | Gift to Journey Church, trip noted as a preference | Yes |
| **General missions gift** | Anyone | Gift to Journey Church missions | Yes |
| **Traveler's own trip payment** | The traveler (or their parent) | Payment, not a gift | **No** |
| **Application deposit** | The applicant | Payment, not a gift | **No** |

- Online gifts go through Stripe Checkout. Card details never touch our server.
- Checks and cash are entered by staff in deposit batches. Closed batches can't be changed.
- Donors can choose to cover the card fee. The fee portion is tracked separately and is **not** credited to the traveler's goal.
- Monthly gifts stop automatically on the trip's start date.

## Exact wording shown to donors today

**On the give page and in every receipt:**

> Gifts are made to Journey Church and are under its full discretion and control. A traveler or trip you name is a preference, not a restriction. If a trip is cancelled, a traveler can't go, or a trip is fully funded, gifts support other missions work.

**On year-end statements, after the gift list:**

> No goods or services were provided in exchange for these contributions, other than intangible religious benefits.

Statements also print the church's legal name, address and EIN, and list each gift's date, amount (less any refund) and method.

## What changed from ManagedMissions

- Removed a "deposit is tax-deductible" option. A deposit for your own trip is a payment, not a gift.
- Voided gifts stay in the records with a reason instead of being deleted.
- Gifts in a year that has been closed out for statements are locked.
- Refunds and card disputes reduce the gift automatically.

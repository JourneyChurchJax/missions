# Medical treatment consent (DRAFT for attorney review)

This is a starting draft for Journey Church's attorney. It is not legal advice and should not be used until an attorney has reviewed it for Florida law and for the countries we travel to (Belize, Israel).

Once approved, it can go into Journey Missions as a "sign" task (with "parent also signs" turned on), so travelers and parents sign it online like the liability waiver.

---

## Consent for Medical Treatment

**Traveler:** ______________________  **Date of birth:** ____________
**Trip:** ______________________  **Dates:** ____________

### Permission to treat

I give permission for the Journey Church trip leaders, and the trip partner's staff, to seek and authorize medical, dental or emergency treatment for the traveler named above if the traveler is injured or becomes ill during the trip, including travel to and from the destination.

This includes first aid, transport to a clinic or hospital, examination, X-rays, anesthesia, surgery, and medicine that a licensed medical provider recommends.

### When we will contact you

Trip leaders will try to reach the emergency contacts listed on the traveler's profile before authorizing treatment, unless a delay would put the traveler at risk.

### Medical information

I confirm that the health information, allergies, medications and dietary needs listed on the traveler's Journey Missions profile are complete and true, and I will update them before the trip if anything changes.

### Costs and insurance

I understand that I am responsible for the cost of any treatment not covered by the trip's travel medical insurance. I will provide the traveler's health insurance information if asked.

## Notes for the attorney

1. **Current waiver:** travelers sign a liability waiver online. The site records the signed document's fingerprint, the signer's name, IP address and time, and emails the signer a copy. Parents confirm with a one-time code sent by email or text.
2. **Minors:** for travelers under 18, a parent or guardian signs too. Is an online parent signature with a one-time code sufficient in Florida?
3. **Foreign treatment:** does consent need specific wording for treatment outside the US?
4. **Medication handling by volunteers:** any limits on leaders giving medication to minors?
5. **Records:** medical details are encrypted and erased 120 days after the trip ends. Is that the right retention period?
6. **Waiver review:** please also review the existing liability waiver text (in the trip's Documents tab).

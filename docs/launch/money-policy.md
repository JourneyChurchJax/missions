# Mission trip funds policy (DRAFT)

Draft for Journey Church leadership and our CPA to review and edit. Not in effect until approved.

## 1. All gifts belong to Journey Church

Gifts for mission trips are gifts to Journey Church. The church has full discretion and control over how they are used. When a donor names a traveler or a trip, that is a preference the church will try to honor, not a restriction.

## 2. How gifts are applied

- Gifts naming a traveler are first applied toward that traveler's trip cost.
- Gifts naming a trip, or no trip, support that trip team or missions in general.
- Once a traveler's cost is fully covered, further gifts naming them go to the same trip team's shared costs. Any amount left after that goes to the general missions fund.

## 3. If a traveler can't go

If a traveler withdraws, is removed from the team, or can't travel for any reason:

- Gifts that named them stay with Journey Church. They are **not refunded** to donors and **not paid** to the traveler.
- Those gifts move to the same trip team's costs, or to the general missions fund if the team is fully funded.
- The traveler's **own payments** (not gifts) are refunded only to the extent the church can recover the money from the trip partner, airline or other vendors. Deposits are non-refundable unless the church cancels the trip.

## 4. If a trip is cancelled

If the church or the trip partner cancels a trip:

- Gifts stay with Journey Church and support other missions work, or the same team's trip if it is rescheduled.
- Travelers' own payments are refunded after the church recovers what it can from vendors. Money already spent and not recoverable can't be refunded.

## 5. Money left over after a trip

Any money left in a trip's budget after all trip costs are paid goes to the general missions fund. It is not divided among travelers or paid back to donors.

## 6. Donor refunds

The church may refund a gift that was made in error (for example, a duplicate charge or the wrong amount) if the donor asks within 30 days. Refunded gifts are removed from the donor's tax statement.

## 7. Receipts

- Donors receive a year-end statement for gifts.
- Travelers' own trip payments and deposits are not gifts and are not receipted.

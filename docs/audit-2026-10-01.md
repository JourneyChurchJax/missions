# Journey Missions: full audit (October 1, 2026)

Read-only audit. Nothing was changed. Four separate reviews (security, bugs, design and accessibility, operations and legal), plus live checks of missions.journeychurch.org, merged into one list with duplicates removed.

**Bottom line:** it looks great and the basics are solid, but it is a well-built prototype, not yet safe for real money, minors' private data, or legal signatures. The biggest problems are sign-in and permissions, the demo switch, money accuracy, and signatures that wouldn't hold up.

**What's done right**
- **Database queries:** every one passes values in safely. No SQL injection.
- **Page output:** escaped consistently. No stored cross-site scripting found.
- **Forms:** every form that changes data has a CSRF token.
- **Uploads:** kept outside the website folder, given random names, and type-checked by the real file contents.
- **Stripe:** Checkout keeps card data off our server, and the webhook checks Stripe's signature.
- **Secrets:** kept out of git.

## CRITICAL: fix before real travelers, parents or donors use it

1. **The live site has demo data turned on, and the public pages show it.**
   - missions.journeychurch.org/thomas and /corey show fake gifts and stories under real people's names.
   - Demo mode is one switch for the whole site, so while it's on, everything uses the demo database:
     - Real applications go into it.
     - Real Planning Center sign-ins go into it.
     - Real Stripe gifts go into it.
   - "Reset demo data" deletes that database, real money records included. Stripe then thinks the gift was delivered and won't resend it.
   - **Needed:** demo should only affect the staff member viewing it. Public pages, applications, sign-in and the Stripe webhook should always use the real database. Turn demo off on the live site now.
2. **A traveler can make themselves staff.** A traveler can change their own email to one on the `staff_emails` list on their profile, sign out and back in, and they're staff with every passport and medical record. (action.php person_save + pco.php is_staff_person)
3. **Planning Center sign-in matches people by email, which can be faked.** Anyone can add someone else's email to their own Planning Center profile and sign in as that person, including staff. Planning Center administrators also automatically become full staff. (auth/pco.php:30-41)
   - **Needed:** match only by Planning Center ID. Staff link people the first time. Staff status comes from a staff-only role list, never from an email.
4. **One shared password is full admin, and it can impersonate anyone.**
   - With it, `/?as=<id>` lets anyone become any traveler and sign waivers, chat, pay and edit profiles as them.
   - Staff can also open a parent's link and sign as the parent.
   - Everything done with the password is recorded as "Adam Hardegree".
   - Guessing it is only slowed slightly: there's no lockout and no log of failed tries.
   - Changing the password doesn't sign out people who are already in, and sessions never expire.
   - **Needed:**
     - Individual sign-in only (Planning Center) in production.
     - Impersonation becomes view-only, staff-only and logged.
     - Session timeouts and a way to cut off someone's access.
5. **Signatures wouldn't hold up legally.**
   - Upgrading the live site created **fake signature records**: every earlier "checked the box" became a signature row with waiver wording those people never saw. Delete or relabel those rows now.
   - Nothing saves a fingerprint (hash) or version of the document that was signed, and staff can replace the file after people have signed it.
   - The signer never gets a copy.
   - A parent's signature is just "whoever has the link". If the link is forwarded, anyone can sign.
   - A minor can change their own birth date to skip the parent signature.
   - Travelers can mark a waiver "done" without signing by sending a hand-made request (`verify_info` / `task_upload` accept any task ID).
   - If a task has no wording, the page shows default text but saves an empty agreement.
   - **Needed:**
     - Save the document's hash and lock that version.
     - Email a copy to the signer.
     - Have parents confirm with a one-time code by email or text.
     - Lock the birth date once someone is on a trip.
     - Only finish signature tasks through an actual signature.
     - Have a lawyer review the waivers, and the missing medical-treatment permission.
6. **No backups.** Everything is one SQLite file plus the uploads folder (passport photos) on shared hosting. There's no backup, off-site copy or tested restore.
   - **Needed:** a nightly safe backup (`sqlite3 .backup`) plus uploads copied to encrypted off-site storage. Keep 30+ days and test a restore.
7. **Permissions are all or nothing, and the permissions screen is fake.**
   - The Leader permissions page saves nothing.
   - The screens say "Leaders see what you allow" and "Medical: leaders and admins only", but that isn't enforced.
   - A Belize leader can't see a student's allergies unless you give them the master password.
   - **Needed:** real roles by trip and by type of data (medical, passport, money).
8. **Minors' medical information and passport numbers are stored in plain text and kept forever.**
   - There's no way to delete a person, no retention rule, no privacy notice, no age check or parent consent on the public application, and no record of who viewed or exported this data.
   - Draft application links show and let anyone edit the passport and medical fields.
   - The outbox stores full emails, including private parent links.
   - **Needed:** encrypt the sensitive fields, purge them after trips, add delete and export for a person, collect parent consent for under-18 applicants, add a privacy notice, and log who views or exports them.
9. **Tax-receipt risk (have a CPA or attorney review this before taking online gifts).**
   - The site says "Gift for Thomas", tells check-givers to write the traveler's name in the memo, and credits 100% of the gift toward that traveler's own trip cost.
   - Parents can give to their own child and get a tax receipt.
   - Nothing says the church controls the funds and the designation is only a preference.
   - There's no policy for leftover money or a traveler who drops out.
   - There's a "Deposit is tax-deductible" checkbox, but a deposit for your own trip isn't deductible.
   - Statements are missing the church's legal name and address as text, and the "intangible religious benefits" wording.

## HIGH

### Money
10. **Bank (ACH) gifts and payments are never recorded.** Stripe's "payment succeeded later" event isn't handled.
11. **Monthly gifts never stop** ("until the trip" isn't set in Stripe), so donors are charged forever.
12. **Monthly gifts will likely break on Stripe's newer setup.** Webhook endpoints created today use the 2025+ API version, where the invoice fields this code reads have moved.
13. **Refunds of monthly gifts are ignored.**
    - A second partial refund on a traveler payment is lost.
    - Disputes and chargebacks aren't handled.
14. **Duplicate gifts can happen.** The "already handled" check runs after the work, and there are no unique indexes or database transactions anywhere.
15. **A donor's covered fee is credited to the traveler.** A $100 gift with the fee covered shows $103.30 raised.
16. **Editing an old gift quietly moves it.** It can come off its trip or traveler (lowering their total) and out of its closed deposit batch.
17. **The books can't be trusted:**
    - Gifts, payments and expenses are hard-deleted with no reason or log.
    - Closed batches can be reopened.
    - Nothing locks gifts after statements go out.
    - Removing a team member leaves their money behind.
    - Re-adding someone shows $0 raised.
    - Totals are a stored number that can drift from the actual gifts.
    - Amounts are stored as decimals instead of whole cents.
18. **Online gifts are dated the day the webhook runs, not the day of the charge.** A gift on Dec 31 can land in the wrong tax year.
19. **Application deposits are handled inconsistently.**
    - A deposit can be paid twice.
    - A deposit marked paid after approval never counts toward the traveler's total.
    - Re-approving someone for another trip adds a second deposit.
20. **A non-early-bird discount saved without a code applies to everyone.**
21. **"Raised this season" (home page) and "Raised for upcoming trips" (Giving page) are calculated differently** and won't always match.

### Wrong data shown to travelers and parents
22. **Travelers, parents and calendars show the earliest active trip, even one that ended years ago.** Trips never become inactive on their own.
23. **Every application creates a new person, even for people already in the system.** Approving then adds the duplicate to the trip, and sign-in picks the old record.
24. **Calendar times are off by hours for Belize and Israel.** Local airport times are treated as New York time.
25. **Copying a trip has three problems:**
    - Dates shift by one day around daylight saving changes.
    - Copied signature tasks point to the old trip's documents, so travelers get "no access".
    - Deleting a document on one trip deletes the file for both.
26. **The countdown shows one day short about four months of the year.**
27. **Signature "done" status drifts from the real signatures.**
    - Staff can check it off in the grid without a signature.
    - Turning "parent also signs" on or off doesn't update people.
    - Deleting a task hides its signature records.
28. **Applicants can see and fill out their own references.** The reference links show on the applicant's thank-you page.
29. **Fundraising pages stay live after edits,** so a traveler can get approved and then change the story or photo to anything.
30. **Travelers can act on other trips' tasks and files,** and can change their legal name and birth date with no staff review. That's the name used for airline tickets.

### Security
31. **No rate limits on public forms:**
    - The apply form can be used to send spam from the church's mail server (the applicant's name goes in the subject, and they control up to 5 reference email addresses).
    - Bots can flood the people list.
    - The give page can be abused for card testing.
    - **Needed:** Turnstile or CAPTCHA, limits per IP and per email, and length limits on fields.
32. **Missing browser security headers:**
    - Nothing stops another site from framing the pages, so they can be clickjacked (including sign and approve buttons).
    - No HTTPS-only (HSTS) or content security policy.
    - The QR code script loads from a CDN with no integrity check.
33. **Open redirect after sign-in using `/\evil.com`.** It can send staff to a fake page to steal the password. `back()` also accepts outside addresses.
34. **Spreadsheet formula injection in every CSV export.** A donor or applicant name like `=HYPERLINK(...)` runs as a formula in Excel.
35. **Private links act as passwords forever.**
    - Parent, application, reference and calendar links never expire or change.
    - Calendar links use `webcal://`, which first sends the private link unencrypted.
    - Parent pages reveal a minor's flights, lodging and schedule.
36. **There's a `config.php` inside `public_html` on the live server.** Secrets should only live in the folder above it.
37. **The deploy uploads `.ftp-deploy-sync-state.json`** (a list of every file name), and `.htaccess` doesn't block dotfiles.
38. **Session gaps:**
    - No strict session mode.
    - Sign-out is a link, so another site can sign you out.
    - Switching views is a link too.
    - The cookie's HTTPS-only setting isn't forced.
39. **Malformed web addresses can crash pages** and may reveal file paths. Error display is never explicitly turned off.
40. **Unapproved page photos can be seen by any signed-in traveler.**
41. **Public fundraising pages show minors' full names, photos and exact travel dates.**
42. **Online gifts are merged into donors by email,** so anyone can attach gifts to someone else's tax statement.

### Operations
43. **Deploys have no safety net.**
    - There are no tests, no automatic error checks, no staging site and no rollback.
    - FTP uploads file by file, so for a while the live site is half old and half new.
    - One typo takes down the whole site, Stripe webhook included.
44. **Database upgrades are unsafe.**
    - They run on regular page loads with no lock and no transaction.
    - The version number is saved before the seeding finishes.
    - Two visitors at once can create duplicate trips.
    - Upgrade seeding rewrites live data.
    - There are no indexes or unique rules anywhere.
45. **Email won't reliably arrive.**
    - PHP mail() from SiteGround probably fails journeychurch.org's email security checks (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), so it goes to spam.
    - Bulk sends (statements, announcements) run inside one page load, will time out partway, and send twice if clicked again.
    - **Needed:** an email service like Postmark or Amazon SES, a send queue, and bounce handling.
46. **Texting isn't compliant.** It needs carrier registration (A2P 10DLC), recorded consent, STOP and HELP handling, and quiet hours. This matters even more because it texts minors.
47. **No error logging, monitoring or alerts.** If the Stripe webhook starts failing, nobody knows until Stripe turns it off.
48. **No real audit log.** "Who did it" is usually the shared name, there's no before and after, and views or exports of medical data aren't recorded.
49. **The Planning Center background check status "incomplete" is saved as Clear.**
50. **Updating a background check resets its request date.**

### Design: the biggest problems
51. **The public give page is broken on phones.** The story column shrinks to nothing. This is the page most donors open from a text.
52. **The traveler home page is broken on phones** in the same way. Documents, Schedule, Fundraising and admin Giving have the same bug. (Inline grid styles override the mobile layout.)
53. **On phones, travelers can't reach their profile** (the avatar is hidden), and the only sign-out is the black preview bar.
54. **Placeholder text shows to travelers and parents on the real Belize trip,** for example "[Airport: add details]", "[TBD] · JAX → BZE", and links that go nowhere.
55. **Fake controls are still live.** The Leader permissions page saves nothing, 6 settings sections are "coming next", and "Finish your next step" does nothing for some tasks.
56. **11 trip tabs don't fit.** On a 1280px laptop "Messages" and "On the trip" are hidden behind a scroll with no hint. They should be grouped (Plan, Logistics, Money, Communicate, On the trip).
57. **Signing requires drawing,** so keyboard users and people who can't draw can't sign. Add a "type your name to sign" option.
58. **"Post" can email and text the whole team and parents with no preview, recipient count or confirmation.** Email is checked by default.
59. **Consequential actions have no confirmation:**
    - Cancelling a trip.
    - Declining an applicant (who also never gets told).
    - Approving (which emails right away).
    - Publishing a form.
    - The demo switch.
60. **Buttons can be double-clicked:** no "working…" state, which leads to duplicate gifts and duplicate emails.
61. **Success messages never go away.** They stay over the bottom of the page, and errors look the same as successes.

## MEDIUM

### Design and accessibility
62. **Ember red is overused.** It's meant to be one accent per screen, but most screens have 2–4. It also means "go", "error" and "danger" at the same time.
63. **591 inline styles** instead of shared classes. That's why phone layouts break and spacing drifts. There's no type scale (about 10 sizes for big numbers) or spacing scale.
64. **Mismatched button heights:** 48px and 44px buttons sit side by side.
65. **Form fields, switches and checkboxes are nearly invisible.** Input outlines are 1.1–1.4 contrast, below the 3:1 rule.
66. **The search box has no visible focus outline.** The sticky header can cover the field you're typing in.
67. **The give page's amount buttons have no visible focus.** The "other amount" box has no label.
68. **Missing autofill hints** (name, email, phone, birthday, address) on the profile and apply forms.
69. **Almost no real headings.** Section titles are styled text, which hurts screen readers.
70. **Progress bars and rings are mislabeled for screen readers.** The fundraising ring says "45 of 100 done".
71. **Small tap targets:** Remove, Unlink, Hide, the ↑↓ buttons, and the photo buttons.
72. **Required fields aren't marked as required.** People find out at the last step. Errors are a generic box with no link to the field.
73. **Editing uses hidden expanding "Edit" links everywhere.** Editing a gift inside a table blows the row apart.
74. **Phone chat:** you scroll past the whole team list to reach the conversation. It should be two screens with a pinned message box.
75. **Hero buttons wrap onto two lines on phones** (seen live).
76. **Sticky headers take about 15% of a phone screen.** The search box disappears on phones with nothing to replace it.
77. **The tasks grid is unusable on phones,** which is exactly what leaders will use in Belize.
78. **Inconsistent words for the same things:**
    - Updates / Messages / Announcements / Chat.
    - Tasks / Checklist / Steps.
    - Giving / Fundraising / Raised.
    - Goals / Milestones.
    - "Not this time" shown to staff.
79. **The give thank-you page says "Gift received" before Stripe confirms it.** "Cover the fee" is pre-checked and the total is never shown.
80. **Stale "coming in phase 5" notes,** a developer note ("Corey and Thomas are real…"), and a hard-coded "Adam" / "AH".
81. **"Your week in Belize" shows each day's last item,** labeled as if it's the first.
82. **Missing empty states** on home "Recent activity", "Coming up", the fundraising pages list, and an empty budget.
83. **Too much black:** the preview bar, hero, tiles, nav, avatars and pills. It reads heavy, not Apple or Airbnb.
84. **The black pill means "good" in some places and is just a label in others.**
85. **Sign-in and Settings show developer instructions to church staff** ("early preview", config.php code).
86. **The header search says "a trip, a person, a donor" but only searches People.**
87. **Settings opens on the Demo data switch.**

### Performance
88. **The Giving page puts a full edit form, listing every donor and traveler, inside each of up to 300 rows.** That can be hundreds of thousands of elements. Load one edit form only when needed, and paginate.
89. **Many repeated database queries per page,** so it gets slower as trips grow (home, overview, documents, applications, tasks).
90. **Images:**
    - Full-size photos are used as thumbnails.
    - There's no resizing on upload.
    - Photos have no caching.
    - The first image on screen is lazy-loaded (it shouldn't be).
    - The logo is a 50KB PNG and should be SVG.
    - Google Fonts loads in a way that blocks the page.
91. **Chat checks for new messages every 8 seconds from every open tab,** even hidden ones. It can also show the same message twice.

### Other bugs
92. **Creating a new person sends you back to a blank "Add a person" form.** It invites duplicates.
93. **A traveler's online payment is silently dropped** if they were removed from the trip before Stripe reported it.
94. **The flight parser fails on:**
    - Real airline emails that use non-breaking spaces (finds 0 flights).
    - 24-hour times after 12:00.
    - Trips that span New Year's.
    - "UNITED 2027" and "ET 11", which are read as fake flights.
95. **MySQL (if you ever switch) would break on:** emoji, text over 255 characters, empty dates, and money returned as text.
96. **The calendar feed doesn't wrap long lines** to the calendar file format's 75-character limit. Outlook may reject it.
97. **Files are left on disk forever:** replaced files, rejected uploads, and demo uploads.
98. **Names with accents (Élise) break the initials** and can show as blank.
99. **A trip with no traveler tasks never shows anyone as "ready".** Under-18-only tasks show for adults in the grid.

## LOW / housekeeping
100. **No print styles** for reports, the guide or signatures. They print the header and buttons.
101. **No link previews (og tags) on the give pages,** so a texted link shows as a bare URL. That costs donations.
102. **Missing icons and metadata:** no apple-touch-icon, theme color or web manifest. The statement page has no viewport tag.
103. **Asset versions (`?v=8`) are bumped by hand.**
104. **Dead code:** the old chat demo, the people side panel, the `deposit_tax` setting, and the unused "Pull trip from Planning Center" action.
105. **The page-address rules differ between two places,** and there's no length limit, so long addresses give "page not found".
106. **`action.php` is one 608-line file with about 80 actions.** HTML, SQL and logic are mixed, statuses are repeated text, and the domain is hard-coded in 5+ places. Hard for another developer to take over.
107. **No setup guide, data dictionary, Stripe or restore runbooks.** The README is out of date and refers to `inc/data.php`, which no longer exists.
108. **Single-church assumptions everywhere,** which matters for the "multi-church someday" idea.
109. **Dark mode isn't supported.**

## Recommended order
1. **Today:** turn off demo on the live site, move `config.php` out of `public_html`, and delete the fake signature rows.
2. **Before travelers sign in:** fix the sign-in takeovers (#2, #3, #4), add real roles (#7), and lock signatures (#5).
3. **Before any online gift:**
   - Fix the money bugs (#10–#21).
   - Get the CPA wording review (#9).
   - Make demo session-only (#1).
4. **Before any broadcast:** set up an email service (#45) and 10DLC texting registration (#46).
5. **Foundation:** backups (#6), deploy checks and staging (#43), safe database upgrades and indexes (#44), logging (#47).
6. **Design pass:** fix phone layouts (#51–#53), remove fakes and placeholders (#54, #55), regroup the trip tabs (#56), and build a real class-based design system (#62–#64).
7. **Privacy:** encryption, retention and parent consent (#8).
